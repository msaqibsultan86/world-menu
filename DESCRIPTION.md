<p>You downloaded a map. Now close the game, hunt for your instance folder, dig through AppData, unzip it into the right place, and hope you didn't nest the folder one level too deep. Launch again — and it isn't there. 😤</p>

<p><b>World Menu ends that.</b> Download a map, hit Import World, pick the folder, play. You never leave the game. 🌍</p>

<hr>

<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/msaqibsultan86/world-menu/main/assets/ico-features.png" alt="" height="42" align="middle">&nbsp;&nbsp;<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/msaqibsultan86/world-menu/main/assets/features.png" alt="Features" height="42" align="middle"></p>

<p>🗺️ <b>Play maps you downloaded, instantly.</b> Adventure maps, parkour, puzzle maps, that CTM pack from 2013 — point at the folder and it's in your world list.</p>

<p>🔁 <b>Move worlds between instances.</b> Built something great in a modpack and want it in your vanilla instance? Import it. Old launcher, new launcher, different version — doesn't matter.</p>

<p>👥 <b>Take worlds from friends.</b> Someone sends you their save, you drop it in without needing a tutorial.</p>

<p>📂 <b>Find your saves in one click.</b> Opens the exact folder <i>this</i> instance uses — no more guessing which of your twelve <code>.minecraft</code> folders is the live one. Perfect for backups, or zipping a world up to send.</p>

<p>✅ <b>It just works.</b> Picked the wrong folder? It tells you instead of breaking. Already have a world with that name? You keep both.</p>
