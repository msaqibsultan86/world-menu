World Menu puts two buttons where they should have been all along — right on the Select World screen. Open your saves folder, or bring a world in from anywhere on your PC, without ever leaving the game. 🌍📂

No more alt-tabbing through AppData to find your saves. No more dragging folders around and hoping you got the right one. 🚀

---

<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/msaqibsultan86/world-menu/main/assets/ico-features.png" alt="" height="42" align="middle">&nbsp;&nbsp;<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/msaqibsultan86/world-menu/main/assets/features.png" alt="Features" height="42" align="middle"></p>

📂 **Open World Folder** — one click opens this instance's `saves` folder in your file manager. Works on Windows, macOS and Linux.

📥 **Import World** — pick any world folder on your PC. World Menu checks it's a real world, copies it into `saves`, and refreshes the list. Your new world is ready to play.

🛡️ **It won't break your saves** — importing a folder that isn't a world tells you so instead of failing silently. If a world with the same name already exists, both are kept. A failed copy cleans up after itself.

🪶 **Client-side and tiny** — nothing to configure, no server install, no impact on your world.
