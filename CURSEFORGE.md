World Menu puts two buttons where they should have been all along — right on the Select World screen. Open your saves folder, or bring a world in from anywhere on your PC, without ever leaving the game.

No more alt-tabbing through AppData to find your saves. No more dragging folders around and hoping you got the right one.

<h2><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/msaqibsultan86/world-menu/main/assets/ico-features.png" alt="" height="24" align="middle"> Features</h2>

<b>Open World Folder</b> — one click opens this instance's <code>saves</code> folder in your file manager. Works on Windows, macOS and Linux.

<b>Import World</b> — pick any world folder on your PC. World Menu checks it's a real world, copies it into <code>saves</code>, and refreshes the list. Your new world is ready to play.

<b>It won't break your saves</b> — importing a folder that isn't a world tells you so instead of failing silently. If a world with the same name already exists, both are kept. A failed copy cleans up after itself.

<b>Client-side and tiny</b> — nothing to configure, no server install, no impact on your world.
